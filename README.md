# 📊 Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

![Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard]()

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of business performance by analyzing:

- Sales
- Profit
- Orders
- Quantity Sold
- Profit Margin
- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Customer Segments
- Sales Channels

The main objective of this project is to transform raw business data into meaningful **KPIs, visualizations, and actionable business insights**.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales across different categories
- Analyze regional profit contribution
- Understand customer segment performance
- Compare different sales channels
- Track sales trends
- Create an interactive and user-friendly dashboard

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 🏆 Product Performance
- **AC** is the top-performing product with approximately **55.56M** in sales.
- Printer has the lowest sales among the products shown, at approximately **47.79M**.

### 📂 Category Analysis
- Appliances generated the highest category sales at approximately **106M**.
- Office Supplies generated approximately **100M**.

### 👥 Customer Segment
- **Consumer** segment generated the highest sales at approximately **49M**.
- Corporate contributed approximately **48M**.
- Small Business contributed approximately **47M**.

### 🚚 Sales Channel
- **Distributor** is the leading sales channel with approximately **172M** in sales.
- Online and Retail Store both generated approximately **171M**.
- Overall performance across the three channels is relatively balanced.

### 🌎 Regional Profit
- Profit contribution is relatively balanced across the five regions.
- Each region contributes approximately 28.13% of total profit.

### 💹 Profitability
- The overall **Profit Margin is 28.13%**, indicating a healthy level of profitability.
